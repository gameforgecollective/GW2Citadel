GW2Citadel v0.1.54.2 - Exit Crash Hotfix

- Fixes the confirmed arcdps_bridge.dll access violation that could crash Guild Wars 2 while exiting.
- Includes a compatibility-preserving patched build of arcdps_bridge v1.0.4.
- Includes a reversible PowerShell fixer that backs up the installed bridge before replacement.
- Verifies the patched bridge with SHA-256 before and after installation.
- Includes the auditable source patch, upstream attribution, and MIT license.

Close Guild Wars 2 before running GW2Citadel-ExitCrash-Fixer.ps1.
